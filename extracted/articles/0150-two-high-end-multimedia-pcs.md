# Two High-End Multimedia PCs

*International What's New*

IPC targets the high-end multimedia computing market with a pair of PCs -- the Helios Mini Tower and the FamilyMagic Excellente -- based on Intel's 200-MHz Pentium Pro processor. Designed for workstation applications, the Helios Mini Tower includes 16 MB of EDO DRAM, upgradable to 512 MB; 2 GB of storage; an eight-speed CD-ROM drive; and an optional 33.6-Kbps fax modem card. The FamilyMagic Excellente, which is designed for home users, includes similar features, plus MPEG-1, 16-bit sound, and wave-table synthesis. With the FamilyMagic Excellente, you can watch video CDs or tapes by simply hooking up a VCR to the system's external input port.
