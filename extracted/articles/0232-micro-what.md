# Micro What?

*Reviews*

The Alps "Micro Dry" process applies dry resin-based inks to paper with a 240-element thermal-transfer head. Like wax-thermal-transfer printers, the MD-4000 melts small dots from an ink-coated ribbon, one pass per color. Unlike wax transfer, resin-thermal transfer doesn't require special paper, and the output doesn't look waxy.
