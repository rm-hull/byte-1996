# FORTRAN 90 Compiler

*International What's New*

The NAG/ACE FORTRAN 90 (SPARC) Compiler provides FORTRAN developers and programmers with enhanced performance and flexibility. It integrates with the CoSy parallel-compilation technology and also accepts HPF extensions. ACE Associated Computer Experts says the new compiler obviates the need for intermediate source-code conversion and produces target-specific final code. Implementations for the PowerPC are expected to begin appearing on the market sometime next year.
