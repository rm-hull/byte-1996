# ISDN-to-PCI-Bus Connection

*What's New*

A Plug and Play COM port for connecting to external ISDN terminal adapters, the LavaLink-PCI Dual-Port Communications Accelerator Board (US$149.99) supports data transfer rates of up to 460.8 Kbps. Each of the board's two COM ports incorporates a 32-byte FIFO buffer and an intelligent I/O processor, allowing Internet and remote-access applications to transfer data at the full bandwidth of the ISDN terminal adapter.
