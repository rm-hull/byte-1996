# Praiseworthy Distortion

*Inbox*

In "Beyond Benchmarking" (August) you state that one of the reasons SPEC92 gave distorted results was that the whole of a program could sometimes fit into the CPU's primary cache. Surely this is not a distorted result but is rather praiseworthy. Our expectations have been submerged by today's monster programs.

"Derrick Simmons

West Sussex, U.K.
