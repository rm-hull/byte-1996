# Multiplatform Smartcard Reader

*International What's New*

Cardman II, a smartcard reader that supports digital signatures, encryption, and the whole range of smartcards currently on the market, connects to a serial port and runs on all Windows, OS/2, and Unix platforms. It includes a set of APIs that allow for read and write operations from standard office applications. You can fasten the device to your PC's keyboard or the case of your notebook.
