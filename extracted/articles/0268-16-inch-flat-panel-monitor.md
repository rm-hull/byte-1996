# 16-inch Flat-Panel Monitor

*What's New*

The PV116SX (under $10,000) is an active-matrix color LCD flat-panel monitor that measures about 3 inches deep and has a resolution of 1280 by 1024 pixels and a diagonal viewing area of 16.1 inches. The monitor provides a 2-million-color palette, on-screen menu controls, and options for finger-touch and pen-input versions.
