# Alpha 21164 Desktop Workstation

*What's New*

The AlphaStation 500/500 ($54,660) supports Digital Unix, Windows NT, and OpenVMS. The workstation comes with 128 to 512 MB of memory; 2 MB of cache; 4 MB of texture memory; a 256-bit-wide memory bus; a PowerStorm 4D60T graphics adapter; a 2-GB hard drive; a 21-inch color monitor; a floppy drive; a quad-speed CD-ROM drive; five storage slots; four PCI bays; one 64-bit PCI expansion slot; twisted-pair and thin-wire Ethernet; and stereo-quality audio.
