# Network-Ready Color Camera

*What's New*

Designed for Internet and intranet applications, the Axis NetEye 200 ($1299) is a self-contained, palm-size camera that attaches directly to a 10-MB Ethernet network. You can use the camera as a replacement for closed-circuit video or a PC with a frame-grabber.
