# Smart Fast Ethernet Switching Hub

*International What's New*

Accton's SmartExtender Fast Ethernet switching hub comes in two models, both of which ensure smooth migration from 10-Mbps to 100-Mbps LANs. The first model, the EH3001-TX, is a Fast Ethernet switch with two 10Base-T/100Base-TX ports. The second model, the ES3002-TF, offers a single 10Base-T/100Base-TX port and another 100Base-FX port. Both models support the 10Base-T, 100Base-TX, and 100Base-FX standards and provide an aggregate bandwidth up to 200 Mbps. The hubs also support auto-negotiation and have a display for network monitoring.
