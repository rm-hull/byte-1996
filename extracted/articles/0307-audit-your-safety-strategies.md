# Audit Your Safety Strategies

*International What's New*

ASM Quick Scan, a program for advanced safety management in the chemical and processing industries, lets you evaluate your organization's safety strategy via a questionnaire. It covers such topics as line responsibility, clear and positive relationships with contractors, and realistic safety targets. The tool helps improve the quality standards of organizations and installations.
