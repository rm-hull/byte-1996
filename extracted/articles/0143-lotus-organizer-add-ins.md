# Lotus Organizer Add-Ins

*International What's New*

The Companions for Lotus Organizer offer travel data for places in Western Europe, including information about airports, postal services, banks and other financial institutions, electricity, business etiquette, restaurants, and hotels. They also cover such topics as economic indicators, information on modern history and government, key industries, and country-specific demographics. You can also access business data about key industries. All the data can be searched and easily updated.
