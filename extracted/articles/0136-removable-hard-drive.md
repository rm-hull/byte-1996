# Removable Hard Drive

*International What's New*

MaxIt is a 3.5-inch removable hard drive for PC or Macintosh workstations that stores up to 540 MB of data. You can install it as a SCSI device under DOS, Windows, Windows 95, or Macintosh System 7. The system achieves data transfer rates up to 10 Mbps and includes a 512-KB cache. The drive measures just 10 by 97.5 by 99 mm.
