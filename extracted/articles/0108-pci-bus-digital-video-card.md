# PCI-Bus Digital Video Card

*What's New*

With the WakeBoard Multimedia Pro (about $1000), you can capture and compress video as an AVI file for playback on a PC. Based on a programmable DSP, so it can perform real-time capture of video, the board comes with 2.5 MB of RAM (2 MB of DRAM and 512 KB of SRAM), S-Video and composite I/O, support for NTSC and PAL, and support for resolutions of from 160 by 120 pixels by 30 fps to 640 by 480 pixels by 15 fps.
