# Multilingual Product Catalog for CD-ROMs

*International What's New*

The EPK electronic product catalog lets you publish product-related data, such as CAD drawings, video clips, and pricing information, in a structured and clear manner on a CD-ROM. It offers purchase forms and e-mail integration and provides several search engines. The system runs on Windows and OS/2 PCs and supports multiple European languages, including English, French, German, and Italian.
