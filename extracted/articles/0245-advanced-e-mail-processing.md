# Advanced E-Mail Processing

*What's New*

Designed for Windows 95 and NT, Re:Ply ($59.95) checks for and downloads e-mail from multiple accounts; generates responses to e-mail according to your criteria; maintains a library of responses or letters that it can automatically plug in; and maintains mailing lists. You can send and receive encrypted e-mail to and from other Re:Ply users; instruct the program to automatically return unwanted e-mail; forward and redirect messages; and send MIME and UUE attachments.
