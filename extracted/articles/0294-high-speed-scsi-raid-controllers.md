# High-Speed SCSI RAID Controllers

*International What's New*

ICP Vortex's new GDT6000 line of fast and wide SCSI disk-array controllers offers complete RAID functionality from level 1 through level 5. These PCI boards achieve data-transmission rates as fast as 40 Mbps in synchronous mode. They have the ability to operate in mixed 8- and 16-bit environments and come in one- and two-channel versions. In addition, drivers for all standard OSes, including Linux, are available.
