# ISDN and LAN Video Communications

*International What's New*

A videoconferencing system that supports the H.320, H.323, and T.120 standards, the ViPA-I2 is an ISDN and LAN multimedia conferencing system for the PC. It allows you to make and receive calls across an analog or ISDN telephone network. An outgoing phone call can be configured as either video- or speech-only. The data-conferencing software lets you share an application and transfer files.
